AUTHOR: Judith Luo
DATE: 2026-04-21
STARS: 1
TITLE: I had a very disappointing experience…
BODY: I had a very disappointing experience with Odie/Nibbles pet insurance. My dog's recent claim for acute bacterial diarrhea was denied as a "pre-existing condition," even though her only prior history was anal sacculitis. After waiting for almost 2 months on my pending claim... The denial letter cites past records of anal gland issues and attempts to reframe them as "bloody diarrhea issues." These are medically distinct conditions. Anal gland disease is not the same as gastrointestinal illness, and no prior GI condition was documented. Their own policy states that a pre-existing condition must involve prior signs or symptoms consistent with the claimed condition. That standard was not met here. Instead, unrelated symptoms were grouped together to justify denial. If you're considering this insurer, be aware that claims may be denied based on broad or questionable interpretations of your pet's medical history.
